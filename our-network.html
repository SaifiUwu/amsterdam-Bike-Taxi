Amsterdam Travel Tips From Our Guides

  • ✦ Check wind speed before going out. Over 20km/h = windy. Over 30km/h = very windy.
  • ✦ 10°C in Amsterdam feels like 5°C — dress warmer than you think.
  • ✦ Calling an ambulance costs €500 — get travel insurance before your trip.
  • ✦ Lost something? Check: ilost.co/org/gemeente-amsterdam
  • ✦ Emergency number: 112 (Police + Ambulance)
  • ✦ Airport taxi: staxi.nl +31 20 705 8888
  • ✦ Our rickshaws have rain curtains — no need to cancel for weather.
