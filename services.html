What We Offer

Our Services

Whether you are looking for a quick ride to your hotel, a scenic tour to hit all the top landmarks, or a custom group arrangement, Amsterdam Bike Taxi is your premium eco-friendly choice in the city center.

A to B Taxi Service

Don't want to walk or squeeze into a crowded tram? We'll pick you up and drop you off anywhere within the city center smoothly. Quick, comfortable, and zero emission.

  • ✔️ Fast dispatch in central ring
  • ✔️ Comfortable spacious seats
  • ✔️ Starts from €10 based on distance

Private Sightseeing Tours

Sit back and relax as your local guide peddles you past the finest canals, hidden courtyards, and famous monuments. Listen to the rich history of Amsterdam.

  • ✔️ From 30 mins to 2 hours
  • ✔️ Historical insights & local tips
  • ✔️ Photo stops upon request

Romantic Evening Rides

Amsterdam is magical at night. We offer evening rides taking you through beautifully illuminated bridges, perfect for couples.

  • ✔️ Quiet, intimate atmosphere
  • ✔️ Blankets provided for colder nights
  • ✔️ Perfect for anniversaries or dates

Group & Event Bookings

Traveling with a large group? We can arrange multiple rickshaws for corporate events, bachelor/bachelorette parties, and special occasions.

  • ✔️ Accommodates up to 20 pax
  • ✔️ Coordinated pickup and drop-off
  • ✔️ Custom routes available
