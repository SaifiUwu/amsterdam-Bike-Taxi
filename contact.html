Our Story

Who We Are

Amsterdam Bike Taxi was founded with a single mission: to provide the most authentic, comfortable, and eco-friendly way to experience the magic of Amsterdam.

Our passionate local guides have been showing visitors the hidden gems, historical canals, and vibrant culture of our city from the comfort of our premium electric pedicabs. We pride ourselves on offering memorable, personal tours rather than a crowded bus experience.
